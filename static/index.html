Scientific Calibration & Bioacoustic Accuracy Notice

• Digital Full Scale (dBFS): Measurements in this toolkit represent relative digital audio levels (0 dBFS = digital clipping ceiling). They reflect uncalibrated digital audio. Conversion to physical underwater sound pressure levels (dB re 1 µPa) requires specific hydrophone voltage sensitivity curves (dBV/µPa) and pre-amplifier gain calibration.
• Physical Signal Analysis: Extracted metrics, frequency distributions, and candidate click sequences quantify physical wave mechanics. They do not decode whale communicative syntax, semantic language meaning, or assert species classification without validated acoustic reference models and biological ground truth.
• Candidate Coda Sequences: Sequences are identified using signal impulse clustering. They represent candidate pulse sequences and must be cross-verified against field annotations before behavioral classification.
